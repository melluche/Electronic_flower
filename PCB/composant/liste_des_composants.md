## Mechanical Structure & Materials

| Material | Description | Usage | 
| ----- | ----- | ----- | 
| Copper Wire | Rigid wire Ø 0.8 mm to 1.0 mm | Petal structure, stems, and conductive links | 
| Brass Tubes | Diameters 1.5 mm / 2.0 mm / 3.0 mm | Central axis, sliding mechanical links | 
| Base | Wooden base or 3D printed enclosure | Structural support and electronics housing | 
| Hardware | Mini M2 screws (e.g., M2 x 4mm) / inserts | Securing mechanics and servo motors | 

## Tools & Consumables

* Soldering iron, solder wire, and soldering flux

* Precision pliers (flat nose, diagonal cutting, round nose)

* Heat shrink tubing, cyanoacrylate glue (Super Glue) or hot glue

* Sandpaper or steel wool (for stripping copper wire before soldering)

Note: The copper wire often serves as a direct electrical conductor to power the LEDs at the top of the flower while maintaining mechanical rigidity. Ensure proper insulation where positive and negative paths might touch during petal movement.
